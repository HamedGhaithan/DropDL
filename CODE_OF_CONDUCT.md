# Code of conduct

DropDL Studio follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want this to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Do:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.

**Don't:** harass anyone, use sexualised language or imagery, make personal or political attacks, troll, or publish anyone's private information without their permission.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and whenever you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@HamedGhaithan](https://github.com/HamedGhaithan), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
